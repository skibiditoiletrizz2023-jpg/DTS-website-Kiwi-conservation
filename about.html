About Kiwi Conservators

About Kiwi Conservation

Kiwi Conservators is a website dedicated to raising awareness about the conservation of kiwi birds in New Zealand. Our mission is to educate the public about the importance of protecting these unique and endangered species, and to provide resources for individuals and organizations to get involved in conservation efforts.

Our Team

Our team consists of passionate conservationists, researchers, and volunteers who are committed to the preservation of kiwi birds. We work closely with local communities, government agencies, and other conservation organizations to implement effective strategies for kiwi conservation.
